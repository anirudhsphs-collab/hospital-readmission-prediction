PRIMARY STAKEHOLDER

General Physician

Patient Assessment and Healthcare Management

Role of the Stakeholder

A general physician evaluates patients, reviews medical histories, assesses health conditions and participates in treatment and discharge planning.

ReAdmit Care is designed as an academic prototype to present estimated readmission risk and model explanations in an accessible format.

Stakeholder Requirements

01. Simplified Data Entry

Reduce the complexity of entering patient information.

02. Risk Visualization

Present estimated readmission probability clearly.

03. Explainability

Display the factors contributing to model predictions.

04. Report Generation

Provide downloadable academic assessment reports.

Expected Benefits

  • Accessible patient risk assessment interface
  • Reduced complexity of model input entry
  • Improved understanding of model predictions
  • Structured presentation of assessment results
These are proposed benefits of the academic prototype, not clinically demonstrated outcomes or confirmed stakeholder interview findings.
