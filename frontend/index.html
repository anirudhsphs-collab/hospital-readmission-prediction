Important Notice

ReAdmit Care is an academic machine learning project intended for educational demonstration only. The model and risk thresholds are not clinically validated. Predictions must not replace professional medical judgment or determine patient treatment decisions. Use fictional patient information only. Google authentication verifies account access, not medical credentials or patient identity.
